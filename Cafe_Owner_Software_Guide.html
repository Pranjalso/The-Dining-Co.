1. Executive Summary

The Dining Co. is a high-speed, contactless dining management platform created to solve the primary friction points of traditional dining: slow table ordering, bill delays, order taking errors, and static paper menus.

Guests simply scan a table QR code to browse an interactive visual menu, apply exclusive offers, and place orders directly from their smartphone — with zero app downloads required. Kitchen staff immediately receive audible notifications and table-wise item breakdowns, enabling rapid food preparation and faster table turnaround.

3. Complete Feature Breakdown

📱 Customer Ordering Experience (Dine-In Web App)

  • Zero App Install: Works immediately in Safari, Chrome, or any mobile browser via table QR code.
  • Categorized Visual Menu: Easy navigation across Coffees, Beverages, Small Bites, Meals, and Desserts.
  • Dietary Badges & Search: Clear Veg/Non-Veg indicators and fast dish lookup.
  • Special Offers & Coupons: Automatic welcome discounts or promo code entry at checkout.
  • Direct Table Routing: Orders automatically map to the guest's seated table number.
  • Feedback & Star Rating: Built-in 5-star rating and comment screen right after meal completion.

🍳 Kitchen & Live Order Management (Admin Dashboard)

  • Real-Time Audio Alerts: Immediate chime notifications whenever a new order arrives.
  • One-Tap Order Status: Seamless transitions from New Order ➔ Preparing ➔ Served.
  • Payment Settlement Screen: Clear tracking of online UPI settlements vs. cash payments at the counter.
  • Order History & Receipts: Detailed historical logs for daily financial reconciliation.

🍽️ Menu & Pricing Manager

  • Instant Item Toggles: Mark any dish as "Sold Out" in 1 tap during rush hours to prevent customer disappointment.
  • Dynamic Price Updates: Adjust pricing, descriptions, and portions anytime.
  • Cloud Photo Hosting: Upload dish images that render crisp and load fast on all customer phones.

🏷️ Promotional Offers & Banner Engine

  • Welcome Popups: Display eye-catching banner offers when guests first open the menu.
  • Custom Discount Rules: Flat discounts, percentage reductions, or minimum cart order requirements.
  • Instant Activation: Enable or disable weekend combos or happy hour offers in one click.

🖨️ Table QR Code Generator

  • Automated Table Mapping: Generate individual QR codes for Table 1, Table 2, Patio, etc.
  • Print-Ready Assets: Download high-resolution QR codes ideal for acrylic stands, table tents, and stickers.

4. Daily Operating Routine

  1. Morning Opening (2 Mins): Log in to the Admin Dashboard. Toggle off any dishes with missing ingredients. Check active offers.
  2. Service & Peak Hours: Guests scan, order, and pay. Staff receives audio chimes, accepts orders, and delivers freshly prepared items.
  3. Closing Shift: Review total order revenue, settled bills, and guest ratings to maintain top service standards.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Do customers have to download an app?
No. Scanning the QR code opens the digital menu instantly in their mobile browser without any friction.
What hardware or equipment do we need?
No expensive proprietary POS hardware needed. Any iPad, Android tablet, phone, or laptop with internet access can run the Admin Dashboard.
How are payments handled?
The platform accommodates both direct online payments (UPI/Card) and traditional "Pay at Counter / Cash" workflows.
Can we add more tables later?
Yes. You can generate and print additional table QR codes directly inside the admin dashboard at any time.
